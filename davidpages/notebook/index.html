::book-pile:: Pocket Notebooks

The pocket notebook is a magical thing...

Whenever I need to jot something down, when I need a piece of paper, when I feel like doodling, I whip it out. Especially with my job, if a kid is ever bored, I just hand them a notebook and a pen. It's so perfect.

These notebooks are filled with notes, doodles, scribbles, and memories. Many are by my hand, but many too are from the various other people in my life... my peers, the other, without whom I would be nothing...
