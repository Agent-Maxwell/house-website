Pocket Notebooks

The pocket notebook is a magical thing. Whenever I need to jot something down, or I just feel like doodling, I whip it out. Especially with my job, if a kid is ever bored, I just hand them a notebook and a pen. It's perfect.

These notebooks are filled with notes, doodles, scribbles, and memories. Many of them are by my hand, but many are from the various people in my life.
