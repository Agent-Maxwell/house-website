Slap

The greatest card game of all time.

This is a very simple reaction game. Play is fast and focused but a full game can go on for a while. It allows anyone who walks up to "slap in" and join the game, which is nifty.

Setup

Divide the deck between the players. You can deal, but I usually just ::eyes:: eyeball it and let people choose their pile.

Players hold their pile facedown, and are not allowed to shuffle or peek at their cards.

::die:: Randomly determine the first lead player.

Object

Get all the cards.

Gameplay

The game is played over a series of rounds.

Round

The lead player begins by playing the top card of their pile into a central face-up stack. (This is a "play.") Then the next player plays on top of the previous card. This continues, one card at a time, in turn order, until someone gets a royal card (JQKA for the purposes of this game). They are now the "challenger." Then, depending on which royal was played, the next player ("the defender") must attempt to get another royal within a certain number of consecutive plays:
Jack: 1 chance
Queen: 2 chances
King: 3 chances
Ace: 4 chances

  • If the defender does get another royal, they immediately stop playing cards, and they become the new challenger. It is now the next player's turn to defend, in the same fashion, depending on what royal was just played.
  • If the player does not get a royal within the specified number of plays, the round ends with the challenger as the winner. They win the whole stack, putting it directly on the bottom of their pile (no shuffling!). They lead the next round.

Slaps

Now for the namesake mechanic... In addition to the normal gameplay above, there is another way for a player to win the stack: if, at any time during play, regardless of what else is happening, there is one of the following patterns at the top of the stack, the first player to slap it wins the pile and leads the next round.
"Double" Two consecutive cards of the same rank (e.g. 55)
"Sandwich" Two cards of the same rank with any single card in between them (e.g. 5J5)

If a card is played on top of a slap before anyone notices it, it's too late.

If multiple people slap at the same time, you can usually tell who won from whose hand is on the bottom. Otherwise just vote or something.

Slapping In

If a spectator slaps a valid stack before any players, they take the stack and join the game.

Running Out of Cards

If someone plays their last card mid-defend while they still have some chances left, the next player takes their place as defender, resuming the defend where it left off, however many chances remain.

Game End

As soon as someone has all the cards, they win!

Details, Variants, Notes...

Etiquette

It's good form to flip your cards towards your opponents as you play them, so you don't see the card until your opponents do. This is a good way to account for the natural advantage you have when slapping your own play (and discourages playing "Ronaldo-style").

::fire:: Burning Cards

This is an optional rule that I usually don't bother with when playing with the kids, but makes slaps feel more meaningful. Bring it out if people start to realize they can just slap all the time.

If a player ever slaps the pile at the wrong time, they must ::fire:: "burn a card:" they put the top card of their pile on the bottom of the stack.

Also, if a player ever plays a card at the wrong time, they must ::fire:: burn that card.

Other Slaps

My sister Audrey sometimes plays "crazy mode" with a few additional slaps:

"Marriage" A Queen next to a King, in either order (e.g. QK, KQ)
"Divorce" A Queen and a King, in either order, with any single card in between them (e.g. Q3K, KAQ)
"Affair" A Queen next to a Jack, in either order (e.g. QJ, JQ)

i.e. Marriages and Divorces = Queens and Kings count as the same card for slaps.
