Jokers

This is my accidental joker collection, I just kept getting decks of cards and putting the jokers in one place cause I don't use them and before I knew it I had like a whole pile

  • The Palazzo ones are cool, there's four for some reason: a black, a green, and two red.
  • Apparently the weird $ symbol is a US, it stands for "United States (playing card company)," so all the Bicycle (aka United States Playing Card Company) decks have that symbol on the jokers.
