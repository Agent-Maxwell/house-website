Extremely powerful medium-range laser shot.

Launch Fighters

Send your crew, two at a time, as fighters that chase the enemy. When they get close enough, they rapid-fire on the enemy. If out in space for too long, they will attempt to return to the mothership before dying.

  • One of the most powerful ships in the game, primarily because of the fighters. Even just two fighters, if they catch up to the enemy, can shred through a healthbar in seconds. A squadron 6 or 8 strong is essentially instant death to your prey.
    • This is just to say don't overdo it. You don't need to waste all your health on fighters. You can very quickly put yourself in one-lucky-shot range if you don't pay attention.
  • If you find yourself falling prey to the Ur-Quan, RUN. Try to kite and shoot down their fighters before they get to you; show them that their investment in fighters is a waste, so they're forced to engage.
