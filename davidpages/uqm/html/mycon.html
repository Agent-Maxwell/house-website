Fungal Spores

Fire a homing Spore Cloud that is concentrated and highly damaging at first, then slowly decays, doing less damage as it diffuses. ::caution:: Self-damage possible.

Regrow Crew

Expend a full battery to regrow some lost crew.

They are volcanic fungus guys. ::mushroom:: They have to terraform planets into molten wastelands in order to live there.

  • ::caution:: WARNING: Thousands of Mycon die each year to their own projectiles. You can help prevent this tragedy: whatever you do, DO NOT fire directly in front of you while moving quickly! Aim to the side to safely eject it out of your path.
  • With a full charge, you can fire two Spore Clouds in quick succession. This can be a decimating finisher at close range.
