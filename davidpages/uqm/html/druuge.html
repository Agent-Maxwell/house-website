Sniper Cannon

Long-range, impactful artillery. High knockback = effective propulsion.

Crew Burn

Sacrifice a crew member to the furnace for a precious chunk of battery charge.

Druuge batteries charge extremely slowly (hence the need for the occasional Crew Burn).

  • Don't hold [::arrow-up::]. Let your recoil accelerate you to speed, then go for sniper shots.
  • In a pinch, a few Sniper Cannon shots (and perhaps a few crew chucked in the furnace) can quickly launch you away from friendly opponents.
