Laser Barrage

Spammable short-range pulse of lasers. Costs zero battery.

Energy Absorb

Hold [special] for Absorb Mode, a state that drains your battery, but incoming damage is prevented and converted into more battery (for more absorption.)

Utwig battery does not charge normally. You can only gain battery by successfully absorbing attacks.

22 points is modest... Utwig can be essentially unkillable in the hands of a skilled pilot.

  • Watch your battery! If you run out, there's no way to get it back; you can no longer absorb damage.
  • Think of Energy Absorb like a parry; time it so that you're only holding the button when you need to.
  • Spam Laser Barrage whenever you're not absorbing.
