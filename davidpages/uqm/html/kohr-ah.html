Attack

Long-range saw blades. Hold down [attack] to shoot farther. They persist in space, slightly guiding toward opponent, until you fire too many, then the oldest ones decay.

TIP: Lead your shots, holding the button down until the blade is blocking your opponent's path.

Special

A short-range, 360° halo of blue fire. Extremely damaging, especially if you can sweep broadside past the enemy. High battery cost: about half your battery bar.

TIP: Watch your battery as you spam blades, so you have a pulse ready in case they approach!
