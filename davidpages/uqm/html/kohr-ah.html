Saw Blades

Long-range: the blade will keep moving as long as you hold down [attack]. They persist in space, guiding slightly when near opponent. When you fire too many, the oldest ones decay; you can have like eight out at a time.

Nova Burst

Short-range, 360° burst of blue fire. Extremely damaging. High battery cost: about half the bar.

Kohr-Ah is my favorite ship... Saw Blades can apply pressure at any range, yet the Nova Burst threatens doom up close. It's a flexible kit with fun applications.

  • Lead your shots, holding down the key until the Blade is in your opponent's path.
  • Watch your battery as you spam Blades... a foolhardy Kohr-Ah will find themselves running on fumes at close quarters. Save enough for a Nova Burst in case they approach!
  • Nova Burst is even deadlier when fired with proper timing. Activate it right before passing the opponent at speed to maximize the number of energy particles that hit them. An optimal hit often oneshots opponents. ::sunglasses::
  • Note that your battery still charges while you're holding [attack].
