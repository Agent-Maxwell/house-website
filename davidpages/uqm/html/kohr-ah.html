Saw Blades

Long-range: the blade will keep moving as long as you hold down [attack]. They persist in space, guiding slightly when near opponent. When you fire too many, the oldest ones decay; you can have like eight out at a time.

Energy Burst

Short-range, 360° burst of blue fire. Extremely damaging. High battery cost: about half the bar.

Kohr-Ah is my favorite ship... The Saw Blades can apply pressure at any range, yet the Energy Burst threatens doom at close range. The battery management is interesting and the gameplay is always dynamic.

  • Lead your shots, holding down the key until the blade is in your opponent's path.
  • Watch your battery as you spam blades... a foolhardy Kohr-Ah will find themselves running on fumes at close quarters. Save enough for an Energy Burst in case they approach!
  • The Energy Burst is even deadlier when fired with proper timing. Activate it right before passing the opponent at speed to maximize the number of energy particles that hit them.
