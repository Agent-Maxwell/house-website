Crystal Shatter

Launch a Crystal; it will keep moving as long as you hold down [attack]. Upon releasing [attack], it shatters, spraying shards in a small radius.

Deploy Dog

Expend a full battery to summon a Dog that chases after the opponent and drains their battery upon contact. It shies away if the opponent faces it.

  • Crystals can also strike opponents directly, dealing a solid chunk of damage.
  • At close quarters, it's sometimes effective to spam Crystals, hoping for random scatter-shot hits.
  • Yes, you can hit and kill your own Dogs—be careful.
  • Note that your battery still charges while a Crystal is out.
