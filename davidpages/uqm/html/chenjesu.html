Shattering Crystals

Launch a crystal; it will keep moving as long as you hold down [attack]. Upon releasing [attack], it shatters, spraying shards in a small radius.

Deploy Dog

Expend a full battery to summon a Dog that chases after the opponent and drains their battery upon contact. It shies away if the opponent faces it.

  • Shattering Crystals can also strike opponents directly, dealing a solid chunk of damage.
  • When in close quarters, it is sometimes effective to spam Shattering Crystals for DPS.
  • You can kill your own Dogs. Be careful.
