Charge Shot

Hold down [attack] to charge up a shot, preventing your battery from charging in the meantime. It will cycle colors, slowly gaining power (red is fully charged). Release to fire, or just ram the opponent with it. Fast projectile, medium range.

Confuse Ray

If it hits the opponent, they will become Confused: they are forced to constantly spin, and their special is disabled. Decent battery cost. Fast projectile, medium range.

  • The Confusion state lasts for a good while. You usually have plenty of time to approach with a charged shot and put them out of their misery.
  • For some opponents (e.g. Utwig, Pkunk), having no special is especially debilitating. Land those Confuse Ray shots when it matters.
  • (Psst! If you're battling a Melnorme and get hit by a Confuse Ray, don't give up!! You can still move (and shoot) by pressing [::arrow-up::] (or [attack]) with proper timing!)
