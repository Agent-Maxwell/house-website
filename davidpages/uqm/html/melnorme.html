Charge Shot

Hold down [attack]. Your battery halts charging, while your shot charges up, cycling colors (red is fully charged). Release to fire (or just ram the opponent with it). Fast projectile, medium range.

Confuse Ray

If it hits the opponent, they will become Confused: they are forced to constantly spin, and their special is disabled. Decent battery cost. Fast projectile, medium range.

  • The Confusion state lasts for a long time. You usually have plenty of time to approach with a charged shot and put them out of their misery.
  • For some opponents especially (e.g. Utwig, Pkunk), having no special is completely debilitating. Land those Confuse Ray hits when it matters.
  • (Psst! If you're battling a Melnorme and get hit by a Confuse Ray, don't give up!! You can still move and shoot by pressing [accelerate] with proper timing!)
