Homing Nukes

Long-range guided Nukes. They turn slowly, so they can be dodged. They disappear after flying for two seconds or so.

Point Defense

As long as you are holding down [special], automatically fire at anything that comes close. Able to destroy some types of incoming projectiles. (Only drains battery when it actually triggers.)
