Homing Nukes

Long-range guided nukes. They disappear after flying for two seconds or so. They turn slowly, so they can be dodged.

Point Defense

As long as you are holding down [special], automatically fire at anything that comes close. Able to destroy some types of incoming projectiles. Only drains battery when it actualler triggers.
