Twin Chainguns

High-DPS, short-range machine guns.

Forcefield

Hold [special] for a forcefield that blocks all incoming damage.

I think crashing into a planet is the only damage type that Forcefield doesn't block. And it doesn't block other effects (like Vux Slowing Pellets or Melnorme Confuse Ray), just damage.

  • Forcefield incoming projectiles until you get close, then shred 'em with your Chainguns at melee range. Don't hold back—nobody out-damages a Yehat at point blank.
  • Note that holding [special] quickly exhausts your small battery; the Forcefield cycles on and off when running on fumes.
