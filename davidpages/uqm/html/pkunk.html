Spark Spray

Shoot some short-range Sparks from the front and sides of the ship.

Insult Charge

Hold [special] to call the opponent mean names. This so entertains the Pkunk that it recharges their battery.

Pkunk batteries do not charge automatically; they must rely on the power of their insults.
When a Pkunk dies, they have a 50%[citation needed] chance to immediately revive with full health. ::devil::

  • No reason not to hold [special] constantly.
  • Spark Spray can be especially effective while spinning. This maneuver is known as the "Death Blossom."
