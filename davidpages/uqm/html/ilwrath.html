Flamethrower

Extremely short-range ship melter.

Cloaking Device

Press [special] to toggle Cloaking. While Cloaked, you are invisible and guided attacks can't find you.

Note that you can't see where you are when Cloaked! But I guarantee you have a better idea than your unsuspecting prey...

  • Cloak. Sneak up. Vaporize 'em.
  • If a guided attack is coming your way, Cloak! Even if your opponent knows where you are, the attack will get lost!
  • Get an intuition for how fast you turn and move (and how the screen zooms lol) so you can navigate while Cloaked.
