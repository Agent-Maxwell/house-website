Glory Device

The third time you press [special], you will explode in a glorious ball of fire. It does lots of damage if you're close to the opponent.

  • In the pilot portrait, you can see how many times you've pressed [special] so you can plan your final blaze of glory.
  • Picking Shofixti can be a great way to cheaply wear down (or destroy!) powerful opponent ships.
