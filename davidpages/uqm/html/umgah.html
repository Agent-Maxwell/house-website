Static Field

Melee-range field that does substantial damage. It can "block" many incoming projectiles. Does not cost any battery (but your battery will not recharge if you're using it).

Reverse Dash

Spend battery to jump backwards incredibly quickly.

Umgah battery does not charge normally. In order to regain battery, you must refrain from using Static Field for a while; then it will recharge all at once.

Very weak ship, but one of my favorites—the strange battery, the short-range attack that also functions as long-range defense, the slow movement paired with the limited ability to close large distances in an instant—it makes for an interesting playstyle.

  • Aim your dash carefully to get right behind them and attack before they can turn around.
  • Also try to reserve battery for an escape if necessary (ideally, you have enough battery to get far away enough that they can't reach you until your battery has recharged again).
