Death Bubbles

Bubbles that wander randomly for a while before despawning. They probably guide a bit.

Comet Form

Transform into a hyperspeed comet and ram into your opponent. This slowly drains your battery, and you revert back to normal when it's fully depleted.

  • Keep in mind that your battery charges pretty slow, and blowing Death Bubbles can quickly exhaust it.
  • Keep one eye on your battery while in Comet Form; get some distance away before it runs out to buy yourself time to recover!
