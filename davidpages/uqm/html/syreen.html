Siren's Call

If near the opponent, makes their crew jump out into space; either player can then pick them up and gain the crew back. If out in space for too long, they die.

The Syreen have room for more crew (42) than they start with (12).

Psychic, human-compatible, their ships are called Penetrators...

  • Circle and farm as much crew as you can before finishing them off with your laser.
