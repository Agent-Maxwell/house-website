Howitzer Turret

Solid medium-range artillery. Fires in whatever direction the turret is currently pointing in.

Aim Howitzer / Deploy Troops

[special] + [::arrow-left::/::arrow-right::]: Aim Howitzer
Angle the howitzer turret relative to your ship; it remains there until you re-aim it.
[special] + [attack]: Deploy Troops
Deploy one crew at a time as Troopers. They chase the enemy, boarding their ship upon contact. Each Trooper can take down a random number of enemy crew before they die. If they successfully kill all the enemy crew, the surviving Troopers return to you. If out in space for too long, they eventually suffocate and disappear.

Orz [special] is like Shift; it has no effect of its own, but modifies what [attack] and [::arrow-left::/::arrow-right::] do.

In the game these guys are the "fingers" of some sort of unfathomable higher-dimensional alien hivemind intersecting with our universe.

  • Orz's special is one of the most powerful attacks, but like any move that invests crew, BE CAREFUL. Only deploy as many Troopers as you need. Remember you don't get them back unless they actually kill the opponent.
  • Aiming the turrent directly backwards can really make people feel stupid for chasing you.
