Neon Beam

Short-range beam with good damage. Exhausts battery quickly.

Slowing Pellets

Small, medium-range pellets that track the enemy. Upon contact, they gunk up their ship, decreasing their acceleration speed and steering speed. The effect stacks according to the number of pellets on them.

The Vux are pathetically slow ::turtle::, so their strategy is to bring opponents down to their level, until they feel lightning-fast by comparison. ::running::

  • Try to land pellets whenever the enemy gets close. Once there's a few stuck to them, there's a point of no return where they can't escape further pellets.
  • When they can't turn fast enough to track you, you can get behind them, stay in their blind spot, and put them out of their misery.
